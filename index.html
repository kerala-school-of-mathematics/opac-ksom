About KSoM Library

The Library at Kerala School of Mathematics (KSoM) plays a central role in supporting advanced learning and cutting-edge research in mathematics and statistics. Envisioned as a state-of-the-art knowledge hub, it serves as a regional repository of mathematical sciences.

  • ✓ Rich collection of pure & applied mathematics literature
  • ✓ Fully automated catalogue powered by Koha Library Software
  • ✓ Campus-wide electronic database access (MathSciNet, JSTOR)
  • ✓ Quiet reading rooms and modern digital facilities
